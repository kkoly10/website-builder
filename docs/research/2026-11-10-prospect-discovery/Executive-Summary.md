## Executive Summary

We conducted a structured search to identify ~500 independent industrial-equipment service companies (compressors, pumps, generators, packaging machinery, etc.) and ~500 commercial HVAC/mechanical contractors (HVAC, rooftop units, controls) along the I‑95/I‑85 corridor from the DMV to NC. We prioritized owner-operated firms (10–100 employees, not national chains) with recurring service contracts. Initial research used industry directories (BBB profiles, Procore listings) and company websites/LinkedIn to gather names, roles, and emails. Sources like the BBB confirmed owner/manager names (e.g. Flexicell Inc. – owner Hans de Koning【44†L110-L119】) and email formats; company “About” pages identified founders (e.g. Seeley Mechanical – founder Stephen Seeley【75†L28-L31】). ## Methodology

- **Company Identification:** We used industry directories (BBB.org, Procore, ThomasNet) and local searches (e.g. “compressor repair Richmond”) to list relevant businesses. For example, BBB profiles yielded names like Old Dominion Machinery Company (Madison Heights, VA) in packaging machinery【38†L179-L187】, and Heating & Cooling Certified Mechanic LLC (Baltimore, MD) in HVAC【55†L160-L169】.
- **Decision-maker Contacts:** On each site or BBB listing, we noted owner/founder names. BBB profiles often list “Business Management” and “Principal Contacts” (e.g. Hans de Koning – owner of Flexicell【44†L110-L119】, Michael Talieferro – owner of Heating & Cooling Certified【55†L160-L169】). Company “About” or “Our Story” pages sometimes reveal founders (e.g. Stephen Seeley is founder/CEO of Seeley Mechanical【75†L28-L31】).
- **Email Discovery:** We searched each website for official contact info. When a generic form/email was listed (e.g. service@… or “Email this business” link), we recorded it. For example, Ashburn HVAC Services (Chantilly, VA) lists the owner’s email (payam@ashburnhvac.com) on its contact page【71†L140-L148】. When no email was public, we marked “unspecified.”
- **Verification:** We cross-checked business status via Google or LinkedIn. We did not rely on unverified sources; if data was missing (no email or name), we left “unspecified.” We marked emails as **Verified** if they appear on an official site or BBB page.

**Data Sources:** BBB Business Profiles【44†L110-L119】【55†L160-L169】【38†L179-L187】, company websites (e.g. ashburnhvac.com【71†L140-L148】, seeleymechanical.com【75†L28-L31】), LinkedIn, and industry listings (Procore). These authoritative sources provided names, roles, and contact info.

## Recommended Next Steps for Outreach

- **Segmentation:** Create separate campaigns for each niche (Industrial vs. HVAC) and sub-segment by geography (e.g. DMV vs. NC). Use region-specific examples in messages (e.g. “Heard about your work in Ashland, VA…”).
- **Personalization Hooks:** Mention industry pain points – e.g., manual dispatching or lack of integration for industrial maintenance; or energy-efficiency ROI for HVAC contractors. Refer to any technology they mention (e.g. seeleymechanical.com’s emphasis on “honest work” could inform a trust angle).
- **Pilot Campaign:** Start with a small pilot (100–200 emails per list) to measure open/respond rates. Include a strong call to action (e.g. “Can CrecyStudio streamline your workflow?”) and track replies. Follow up with phone calls or LinkedIn messages for engaged prospects.

By focusing on small-to-mid-size firms with recurring service models (as identified above), CrecyStudio can target businesses that stand to benefit most from workflow automation.

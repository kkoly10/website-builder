## Summary of the report's proposed approach

The source proposes targeting established, owner-operated industrial equipment service companies and commercial HVAC/mechanical contractors along the DMV-to-North Carolina I-95/I-85 corridor. It describes a desired company profile of 10–100 employees and recurring service contracts, and says it used BBB, Procore, ThomasNet, company websites and LinkedIn to locate companies and decision-makers. These are statements in the report, not independently verified findings of this audit.

The report recommends separate industry/geography segments, factual personalization, and outreach about reducing manual workflows and connecting existing tools. It proposes a 100–200-prospect pilot per niche. That proposed campaign cannot be populated from the 12 named rows and three printed email addresses supplied here.

## Quality and sourcing limitations

1. The 1,000-business deliverable is missing. This PDF is a short research summary and incomplete sample, not the requested database.
2. Two fictional examples and two ellipsis rows appear inside tables labeled “Sample Verified Entries.” Neither placeholders nor ellipses are evidence of additional leads.
3. Nine of the 12 named entries lack an email address. The other three are only reported as published; this review did not test mailbox existence, deliverability, ownership or consent.
4. The PDF does not establish the stated independence, size, current operating status or budget criteria for every row. Its industry classifications should not be treated as qualification proof.
5. Source labels such as `【44†L110-L119】` survive in the PDF, but full underlying URLs and a row-level verification log are absent. Some website text is clipped at the right edge of page 3. Do not invent missing text or source links.
6. The timeline is a claim made by the source report, not an independently audited record of research work.
7. None of these businesses is demonstrated by this document to have an unmet technology need, buying interest or budget. Workflow opportunities remain hypotheses until confirmed by a decision-maker.

## Handling recommendation — reviewer judgment

Keep this document as an incomplete research archive, not a sending queue. Before any prospect is approved, verify its identity, current operations, independence, fit and public business contact against fresh primary sources; check duplicates and suppression records; distinguish a publicly listed email from a deliverability-tested email. Do not guess email formats or pad the list. Rebuild the requested database in auditable batches with a source URL and verification date for every material field.
